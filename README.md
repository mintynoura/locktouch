# Locktouch

A small mod that makes Vaults easier to loot by adding a few extra layers of gambling.
Made for a creative jam hosted by my friend Tau!

## Lockpicks

Lockpicks are craftable items that have a chance of opening a Vault, but break upon use. By default, the chance is 30%.

## Lockpicking Charm

The Lockpicking Charm is an item that allows you to reuse Trial Keys or Lockpicks occasionally. When held in the offhand, it grants a 40% chance for the Key/Lockpick to not get consumed upon use.

Lockpicking Charms are rarely dropped by Pillagers, but may also be found by brushing Suspicious Sand blocks in Desert Temples.
